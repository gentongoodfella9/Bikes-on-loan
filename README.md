# Bikes on Loan Website

## Publish on GitHub Pages
1. Upload `index.html`, `style.css`, and `script.js` to your GitHub repository.
2. Open **Settings → Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
4. Choose **main** and **/(root)**, then click **Save**.
5. Wait a short time and open the Pages URL shown by GitHub.

## Before publishing
- Replace `+254 7XX XXX XXX` with your real phone number.
- Replace the WhatsApp number `254700000000` with your real WhatsApp number.
- Replace sample bike names/prices with your actual bikes.
- Replace the emoji placeholders with your own bike photos if desired.
